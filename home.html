Data Engineering & Analytics Architecture

Four-stage automated pipeline transforming noisy raw sensor streams into research-grade analytical datasets.

Stage 01

Multi-Sensor Ingestion

Hourly telemetry capture from laser optical particle counters, electrochemical gas probes (NO₂, CO, SO₂), and meteorology instruments.

Stage 02

Outlier Filtering (IQR)

Rolling 24-hour interquartile range (IQR) detection excises 2,573 non-physical optical dust spikes without eliminating genuine pollution peaks.

Stage 03

Zero-Drift & Imputation

Corrects 942 negative baseline sensor drifts. Replaces ADC telemetry freezes with iterative cubic splines to achieve 100% data completeness.

Stage 04

Statistical Modeling

Rigorous ANOVA, Kruskal-Wallis, and post-hoc Mann-Whitney U testing to evaluate spatial zoning effects and wet deposition aerosol scavenging.
